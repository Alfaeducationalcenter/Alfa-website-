About ALFA EDUCATIONAL CENTER

Alfa ni kituo bora kwa ajili ya kumuandaa mwanafunzi kwa hatua nyingine ya masomo, kielimu, tabia, uongozi, ujuzi na ugunduzi wa vitu vipya pamoja na kuendeleza kizazi.

🎯 Mission

To build ability, creativity and knowledge.

👁️ Vision

Alfa Educational Center kibaki kituo bora kwa ajili ya kuwajengea wanafunzi uwezo, ubunifu na maarifa zaidi.

MOTTO

Education for training mind to think

📖

Academic Excellence

Discover programmes designed to develop knowledge, practical skills, critical thinking, and professional competence. Pre-Form One: B/Mathematics, Physics, Biology, Chemistry, Kiswahili, Geography, History, English, Historia ya Tanzania na Maadili, Business Studies. Form II-IV: B/Mathematics, Physics, Biology, Chemistry, Kiswahili, Geography, History, English. Form V-VI: CBG, PCB, HKL, HGK, HGL na PCM.
