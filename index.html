Why Choose ALFA Educational Center of Salawe?

If you have high academic potential and you are looking for a center with an international ethos and a vibrant community, then ALFA EDUCATIONAL CENTER is the ideal choice for you. The Center has many special features that combine to give it a distinct character.

Vibrant, Friendly Community

ALFA has about 350+ students, together with qualified teaching and non-teaching members of staff. The Center ensures that its members share the learning facilities in an intimate and friendly community atmosphere at Mhangu B, Salawe-Shinyanga.

— BACKGROUND

Rooted in history, building the future

ALFA EDUCATIONAL CENTER (AEC) Salawe-Shinyanga was established in 2023. It is the successor of the vision to provide extra education for Pre-Form One, O-Level and A-Level students. The center was founded by dedicated teachers under the initiative of Mwl. Emmanuel Mashauri Shinje.

The Center extends over Mhangu B area, near Mhangu B Primary School, Salawe, Shinyanga. It lies off the Shinyanga-Mwanza road. As part of Tanzania's central zone, Salawe area is noted for its community support for education. Salawe can be reached from other parts of Shinyanga by highways.
